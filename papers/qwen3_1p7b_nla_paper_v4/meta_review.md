# Meta-Review: Universal Activation Oracle — Reading Latent Behaviour Across Heterogeneous LLMs (v4)

Three independent reviewers (alfa, bravo, charlie) assessed this revised manuscript. All three reached the same verdict — Major revision — and converged on the same small set of load-bearing problems. This version has clearly responded to prior feedback (the n=500 TOST is now run, an n≥80 positives floor was added, GlobalOpinionQA construct-matching was done), but the reviewers find that several headline claims still outrun the evidence and that the reproducibility infrastructure remains invisible at review time.

## Per-reviewer verdicts

- **alfa** — Major revision: Novelty relative to concurrent UAV [2605.25903] needs restructuring; the synthetic-to-real gap (0.55) must be elevated from limitations to the abstract; the 0.642 wrong-label-shuffle residual needs a stronger control; and the unverifiable pre-registration combined with post-hoc corrections (n≥80 floor that "changed the scientific conclusion") weakens the paper's key methodological selling point.
- **bravo** — Major revision: Blocking concern is reproducibility — the primary FVE point estimate (0.874) comes from a run whose logs are lost, the anonymized code is inaccessible during review, and the training sub-categories (hard-negative, dir-positive) comprising ~half of audit supervision are never defined; plus a Qwen-family homophily confound and the unresolved 0.642 fine-tuning artifact.
- **charlie** — Major revision: The external-validity gap (real-world mean AUROC 0.55 vs. abstract's "0.95–0.99"), the inconsistent use of "zero-shot" for predominantly in-family generalization, the unverifiable pre-registration, and the absence of a transcript-only text-classification baseline require revision — though none need new training runs.

## Common concerns

**1. Synthetic-to-real gap (real AUROC ~0.55) is buried while organism-only 0.95–0.99 leads the abstract (alfa, bravo, charlie).** Unanimous and arguably the central objection. The headline auditing numbers are measured only on synthetic model organisms; construct-matched real benchmarks (Table 13) average 0.554, with lgbt-negative *below* chance (0.403). charlie: *"A mean real-world AUROC of 0.554 is operationally indistinguishable from chance on most subgroups."* All three want the organism-only scope and the 0.55 figure named in the abstract, not deferred to §4.9.

**2. "Zero-shot" is applied to predominantly in-family generalization (alfa, bravo, charlie).** Six of ten Figure 4 held-out concepts (chinese, muslim, atomic, chocolate, decimal, sports) are members of trained families scoring ~1.0; the genuinely out-of-family boundary (Table 9, N=80) has two of five at/below chance. The paper concedes this in §4.4 body text but the abstract/§1/headings keep the unqualified "zero-shot mean AUROC 0.94." All three want "zero-shot" reserved for the out-of-family Table 9 results.

**3. The 0.642 cross-category-shuffle residual indicates an unresolved fine-tuning/organism-construction artifact (alfa, bravo, charlie).** Wrong-concept injection only drops AUROC 0.966→0.642 — ~half the above-chance signal survives label swap, consistent with detecting "this model was fine-tuned/prompted by our pipeline" rather than concept-specific content. bravo and charlie both propose the same decisive control: run the shuffle separately on prompted-only (no weight shift) vs. fine-tuned organisms, and/or include neutrally-fine-tuned organisms; if those score above chance the artifact is confirmed. alfa proposes the cleaner same-concept-clean-negatives vs. different-concept-organism-positives ablation.

**4. Verbalization "parity" / TOST framing overstates a result that is behind on neutral metrics, with an unjustified ±0.10 margin (alfa, bravo, charlie).** The new n=500 TOST is credited as the right analysis, but all three flag that (a) the abstract's "beats a 4× larger specialist" contradicts the body's "parity, not a win"; (b) equivalence within a ±0.10 win-rate margin is author-chosen and generous — ±0.05 might not hold; and (c) the GPT-4o-vs-raw-passage score is 41% (significantly below 50%). bravo additionally flags an unreconciled tension between the n=100 41% and the n=500 48.9% (a 7.9pp swing requiring the extra 400 passages to average ~52%).

**5. Pre-registration (SPLIT.md) is unverifiable under double-blind review (alfa, bravo, charlie).** The selection-bias protection rests entirely on a withheld anonymized file across an ~8-version development history. alfa sharpens it: the n≥80 floor (which "changed the scientific conclusion" for medical-advice, 0.333→0.441) was a post-hoc fix in response to review, so it was not in any pre-registration. All three want a timestamped SPLIT.md / SHA-256 / public commit hash or OSF registration at submission.

**6. rugpt3-large held-out FVE 0.995 outlier and depth-fraction 0.5 are flagged but unablated (alfa, bravo, charlie minor).** All three want the 0.995 outlier visually separated and the excluded held-out mean (0.738) in the main text/caption; alfa and charlie additionally note depth-fraction 0.5 is fixed across all 18 architectures with no FVE sensitivity analysis.

## Unique concerns

- **Novelty vs. concurrent UAV [2605.25903] is asserted, not numerically established (alfa).** Contribution (1) overlaps UAV's cross-architecture verbalization; the auditing oracle should be foregrounded as the primary novel contribution, with a design-axes comparison table.
- **No per-model linear-probe baseline for auditing — universality cannot be isolated from multi-task training (alfa).** A logistic/MLP probe trained directly on Llama-3-8B activations would show whether the universal design does any work for the auditing result.
- **Qwen-family architectural homophily: oracle pool is 5/7 Qwen and "held-out" Qwen3.5-4B is near-identical to trained Qwen3-4B (bravo).** Results should be stratified by family distance; Qwen3.5-4B may cluster with within-family rather than out-of-family targets.
- **Lost-logs delta (0.874 single-run vs 0.851±0.001 three-seed) is unexplained and exceeds seed variance (bravo).** Either the recipe changed or the original run was a favourable outlier.
- **Undefined training sub-categories (hard-negative, dir-positive ≈ 50% of audit supervision) make the data composition irreproducible (bravo).**
- **No transcript-only text-classification baseline (charlie).** Zeroing the activation→chance rules out prompt priors but not that biased transcripts are lexically separable; a transcript-only classifier is the missing control.
- **Scale is an uncharacterized failure axis (charlie).** Verbalization already degrades at 27B (0.455 vs 0.558); no target >7B in training, nothing tested ≥70B, so "universal" is scale-bounded and undisclosed as such.
- **Several presentation bugs flagged by multiple reviewers (all):** dangling "Section 12" cross-reference (bravo, charlie), internal filenames in Figure 4 caption / eval_*.json (alfa, bravo), reliance on a LessWrong post [8] and double-anonymous preprints [2]/[3] for load-bearing claims (alfa).

## Ranking

1. **bravo** — Most useful. Adversarial-reproducibility lens surfaces the highest-impact net-new findings (the undefined hard-negative/dir-positive sub-mix that makes ~half the audit data irreproducible, the Qwen-family homophily confound on the "held-out" set, and the unexplained 0.874→0.851 lost-logs delta) while still landing the shared confound and overclaiming concerns, with the most precise proposed controls.
2. **charlie** — Close second. Cleanest articulation of the external-validity gap and the zero-shot/in-family conflation, and uniquely identifies the missing transcript-only baseline (the single most decisive cheap experiment) and the unaddressed scale axis; loses the top slot only because several points overlap the others.
3. **alfa** — Strong and useful. Owns the novelty-vs-UAV restructuring and the missing per-model probe baseline, and is sharpest on the pre-registration-vs-post-hoc-correction tension; ranks third only because its core results-level objections (synthetic-real, shuffle residual, TOST margin) substantially overlap the other two.

## Verdict synthesis

**Major revision** — unanimous, and converging tightly on what must change. This is a genuinely improved revision: the reviewers credit the n=500 TOST, the n≥80 floor, the construct-matched GlobalOpinionQA re-run, and the unusual self-criticism (every reviewer notes the paper is honest about its own limits). But honesty in §4.9 is not the same as calibrated headline claims, and all three reviewers independently land on the same gap between framing and evidence: the abstract leads with organism-only 0.95–0.99 while the deployment-relevant real-world number is 0.55; "zero-shot" labels what is mostly in-family generalization; "parity"/"beats the specialist" survives only under a teacher-biased metric and a generous self-chosen ±0.10 TOST margin; and the 0.642 shuffle residual leaves the fine-tuning-artifact hypothesis live. Layered on top is a reproducibility picture invisible to reviewers (withheld code, lost original logs, undefined sub-categories, unverifiable pre-registration). Critically, the reviewers agree that almost none of the fixes require new training runs — they are abstract/claim recalibration, one or two cheap controls (prompted-vs-fine-tuned shuffle split, transcript-only baseline, per-model probe), CIs on Table 8, defined sub-categories, and a timestamped SPLIT.md. The underlying contribution (closed-form linear-adapter universal reader + joint-trained auditing oracle) is regarded as real and publishable; the revision is about scoping the claims to the evidence and making the verification surface visible.

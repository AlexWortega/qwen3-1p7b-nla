# Meta-Review: "Universal Activation Oracle: Reading Latent Behaviour Across Heterogeneous LLMs" (rev. 3)

Synthesis of three independent reviews (alfa, bravo, charlie). This is the third revision (18 pp). All three reviewers explicitly credit the paper's growing honesty — it now discloses the v15→v22 version search, hedges verbalization to "parity" under an independent judge, expands the NLA comparison to three released targets, and reports the rugpt3-excluded FVE. The criticism has converged: the same two or three structural problems recur across all three reviews, and the recommended fixes are now mostly reporting/ablation changes rather than new large-scale experiments.

## Per-reviewer verdicts

- **alfa** — Major revision: Two foundational design problems block the headline claims — the single "held-out" oracle architecture (Llama-3-8B) shares the Llama-style structural family with SmolLM2/3 already in the verbalization training pool, and the uncorrected v15→v22 search over a fixed held-out split makes the AUROC/zero-shot numbers best-of-k draws rather than unbiased estimates; compounded by an underpowered (n=100) verbalization comparison and a synthetic-to-real gap (0.60) that is a construct-validity failure, not just "scope."
- **bravo** — Major revision: The "zero-shot held-out concepts" set visibly overlaps training (chinese, muslim appear in both, both at AUROC 1.0) with no defined boundary; the shuffle causal ablation is underpowered and doesn't rule out surface-text-style leakage; the version-search disclosure is honest but unquantified; and the field's text-inversion prior work (arXiv:2509.13316) is uncited with no verbalization-zeroing ablation to rule it out.
- **charlie** — Major revision: The uncorrected version search over a fixed held-out set inflates every headline held-out number, and the missing verbalization-zeroing ablation leaves the central claim that the trunk *reads activations rather than the input passage text* unsubstantiated for verbalization — exactly the gap arXiv:2509.13316 documents and this paper neither cites nor tests.

## Common concerns

- **Uncorrected v15→v22 version search over a fixed held-out set inflates every headline number.** *(alfa, bravo, charlie — unanimous)* Honestly disclosed but not fixed; 8 versions selected against the same Llama-3-8B / 5-architecture / held-out-concept splits make 0.988 and 0.918 best-of-k maxima. charlie: *"With eight versions × ten held-out concepts, finding five at exactly 1.0 AUROC by chance is a concrete alternative hypothesis … no p-values are reported at all."* Fix: a strict test split never seen by any version, or report the held-out AUROC spread across the last ≥3 versions.

- **"Zero-shot held-out" concepts overlap the training set; the in/out-of-family boundary is post-hoc and undefined.** *(alfa, bravo, charlie)* chinese and muslim appear in both the training social-bias list (§3.2) and Figure 4's "zero-shot" set, both at AUROC 1.0; the in-family taxonomy is inferred from outcomes (success⇒in-family, failure⇒out-of-family), making it unfalsifiable. bravo: *"Two of the five 1.0-AUROC 'zero-shot' cells may not be zero-shot."* alfa adds gender is similarly listed as a trained social bias yet the abstract claims it was detected "with no positive example seen." Fix: a pre-specified per-concept table (name, direction, training membership Y/N, sub-type distinction).

- **The single oracle held-out architecture (Llama-3-8B) is not truly unseen and is Qwen/Llama-adjacent.** *(alfa, bravo via pool composition)* The trunk's LoRA co-trained on SmolLM2/3 ("Llama-style: GQA, RoPE, SwiGLU") — the same structural signature as Llama-3-8B — so the encoder already learned the Llama manifold; the oracle pool is also 5/7 Qwen. alfa: transfer *"could be because the enc_M adapters all map into the same Qwen-shaped shared space."* Fix: hold out a genuinely novel family (Mamba/SSM, MoE, or the LFM2 already in the reconstruction pool) and report per-concept breakdowns.

- **The shuffle causal ablation doesn't rule out the fine-tune-artifact / surface-style confound.** *(alfa, bravo, charlie)* Zero/noise collapse AUROC to 0.50 (good) but "shuffle" (real activation, wrong transcript) sits at 0.525 with no CI; if shuffled activations are still drawn from the biased distribution, a biased-vs-neutral fine-tune signature would produce exactly this. charlie's decisive fix: *"add a cross-category shuffle — inject a real encoded activation from the wrong label … if AUROC inverts or stays at 0.5, the oracle reads the specific activation content."* bravo proposes a within-concept / paraphrase shuffle to the same end.

- **Verbalization "parity" rests on n=100 with CIs spanning near-chance; no equivalence test.** *(alfa, bravo, charlie)* GPT-4o judge gives 49% vs. teacher [42,62]% and 46% vs. raw passage [36,55]% — neither excludes 50%, and the data are consistent with modest inferiority. charlie: *"a finding of no significant difference on n=100 is not evidence of equivalence; it is evidence of insufficient power."* Fix: n≥500 and a two-sided equivalence (TOST) test. (bravo also notes the reported [42,62]% CI is miscalibrated vs. a Wilson interval of ~[39,59]%.)

- **No verbalization-zeroing ablation / text-inversion threat unaddressed; arXiv:2509.13316 uncited.** *(bravo, charlie)* The AV input contains both the activation and the full passage text, so the trunk could be summarizing the passage with the activation decorative; the causal ablation exists only for auditing. charlie calls this *"the single most important missing experiment"*: zero the injected activation and measure cosine-to-teacher — if it stays ~0.5–0.6, the verbalizer is reading context, not the activation.

- **Synthetic-to-real transfer (mean AUROC 0.60, chinese inverts to 0.40) is a construct-validity problem, not just scope; high clean-FP.** *(alfa, bravo)* The abstract's "detects social and political biases at 0.95–0.99" reads as real-world capability while real benchmarks (ToxiGen/BBQ) give 0.60 and held-out clean-FP is 0.14–0.24 (one-in-four false alarms). alfa: reframe to *"detects activations associated with organism-planted social/political framing"* and remove deployment-grade safety-auditing language from the abstract.

## Unique concerns

- **No quantitative head-to-head against the most direct concurrent competitor, UAV (arXiv:2605.25903).** *(bravo, charlie)* Differences (closed-form vs. trained adapters) are asserted as architecture choices, not demonstrated advantages; a shared-pool FVE/cosine comparison is needed.
- **The "2.3× the per-model NLA FVE" baseline is cross-paper and uncontrolled.** *(bravo)* The NLA 0.38 comes from a different depth/corpus/normalization/trunk; rerun on the same FineWeb-Edu corpus at mean-pool depth 0.5 or drop the ratio.
- **Karvonen et al. head-to-head (0.887 vs. 0.859) mixes AUROC with accuracy at an unspecified threshold and unspecified n.** *(bravo)* Report AUROC for both at the same operating point.
- **The 4-bit-quantized target in the LatentQA comparison is buried and its distributional effect is unknown.** *(charlie)* Mid-layer residual geometry shifts under 4-bit; report target precision per table and re-run matched.
- **Model-organism construction underspecified (fine-tune vs. prompt-induced not separated).** *(charlie)* If organisms are prompt-induced, the oracle may detect a biased context token, not a weight-level latent property — decisive for the safety claim; report prompted vs. fine-tuned separately.
- **Oracle training pool is 5/7 Qwen, so "architecture-invariant" features may be Qwen-specific geometry.** *(alfa)* Ablate with a non-Qwen-dominated pool.
- **Table 3 mean-FVE arithmetic appears inconsistent** (0.874 headline vs. ~0.863–0.877 recomputed from rows). *(charlie)*

## Ranking

1. **charlie** — Lands the single most important missing experiment (the verbalization-zeroing ablation that would settle whether the trunk reads the activation or the passage), pins it to the uncited field-defining prior work (2509.13316), and supplies the decisive cross-category-shuffle control plus the buried-4-bit-target catch. Maximally adversarial but every objection is constructive and load-bearing.
2. **bravo** — Most forensic on the zero-shot overlap (names chinese/muslim/gender concretely in both sets), independently derives the same text-inversion gap and version-search inflation, and adds the strongest baseline critiques (uncontrolled 2.3× ratio, mismatched-metric Karvonen comparison, missing UAV head-to-head); steelman-then-press framing keeps the credit fair.
3. **alfa** — A rigorous adversarial-design review whose unique contributions (the Llama-family contamination of the "held-out" architecture, the Qwen-dominated pool undermining architecture-invariance, and reframing the 0.60 real-source gap as construct-validity rather than scope) are sharp; ranked third only because its statistical and zero-shot points overlap heavily with the other two.

## Verdict synthesis

**Major revision** — unanimous, and the consensus is unusually coherent: the three reviews independently converge on the same short list of decisive issues, which both raises confidence in the verdict and makes the path to acceptance concrete. All three reviewers credit the manuscript's transparency and judge the core contributions (closed-form universal linear adapters, the direct dec_M refit, the joint-training and breadth-as-lever findings, and a genuinely well-designed auditing causal ablation) as real and interesting. The blockers are now narrow and largely addressable by reporting and small ablations rather than new systems: (1) the uncorrected v15→v22 search over fixed held-out splits inflates every headline held-out number and must be repaired with a strict never-seen test split or cross-version variance reporting; (2) the "zero-shot" concept set overlaps training (chinese/muslim/gender) with an undefined, post-hoc family boundary; (3) the sole oracle held-out architecture is Llama-family-adjacent to the training pool and the pool is Qwen-dominated, so "architecture-invariant transfer" is not yet established; (4) there is no verbalization-zeroing ablation, leaving the field's central text-inversion threat (2509.13316, uncited) unaddressed for the verbalization claim; (5) the "parity" verbalization result is underpowered (n=100, CIs touching chance) and needs an equivalence test at n≥500; and (6) the synthetic-to-real gap (0.60, with a 0.40 inversion and 0.14–0.24 clean-FP) should be reframed as a construct-validity limitation with safety-auditing language removed from the abstract. None of these are fatal to the idea and the disclosure culture is exemplary, so Major revision rather than Reject is clearly correct — but the headline AUROC, zero-shot, and parity claims cannot stand as written until the version-search inflation and the two missing controls (cross-category shuffle; verbalization-zeroing) are resolved.

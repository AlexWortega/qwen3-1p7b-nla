# KitFT specialist AVs vs v8 universal AV — full per-passage tables

Two head-to-head experiments comparing our v8 universal AV (Qwen3-1.7B + LoRA,
~1.7B params, one shared AV across 18+ source models) against KitFT's per-model
NLA specialists (full fine-tunes of each source model itself).

**What the AVs interpret:** for each passage, the source model is forward-passed
through the passage. The activation captured is **mean-pooled over all content
tokens** at the layer KitFT trained for (Qwen2.5-7B L20 / Gemma3-12B L32). Both
AVs receive that single d-dim vector as input and emit a natural-language
description. (No per-token interpretation here — the activation summarizes the
whole passage. The v8 stack also supports per-position interpretation; that
mode is documented in `scripts/extract_per_position_multi.py` but not used in
this comparison.)

The GOLD column is the teacher z (OpenRouter `anthropic/claude-sonnet-4-6`),
which describes what the passage is about. Cosine similarity uses mean-pool
`sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2`. The LLM-as-Judge is Sonnet 4.6 with
pairwise prompts and A/B order randomized per passage to mitigate position bias.

## Qwen2.5-7B layer 20  (v8 TRAINED tag)

v8 was **trained** on Qwen2.5-7B activations as one of 13 source tags in the pool. KitFT's `nla-qwen2.5-7b-L20-av` is a full FT of Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (~7B params, no LoRA), trained specifically for this model's L20 activations.

**Source activation:** mean-pool over all content tokens (no single "target" token — both AVs interpret the average representation of the passage at the named layer).

- v8 cos vs gold: **0.6088**

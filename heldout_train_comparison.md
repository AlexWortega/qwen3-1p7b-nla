# KitFT specialist AVs vs v8 universal AV — full per-passage tables

Two head-to-head experiments comparing our v8 universal AV (Qwen3-1.7B + LoRA,
~1.7B params, one shared AV for 18+ source models) against KitFT's per-model
NLA specialists (full fine-tunes of the source model itself).

Both AVs read activations extracted at the layer KitFT trained for. The
teacher z (gold) is generated by OpenRouter (Sonnet 4.6 for Qwen2.5-7B run,
Sonnet 4.6 for Gemma3-12B run). Cosine similarity is computed via mean-pool
`all-MiniLM-L6-v2`. LLM-as-Judge is Sonnet 4.6 pairwise, A/B order randomized
per passage to mitigate position bias.

Tables are sorted by Δcos = cos_v8 − cos_kitft (most v8-favoring rows first).

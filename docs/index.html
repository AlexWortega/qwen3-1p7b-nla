Universal NLA · Activation Oracle · v24

One architecture to read them all.

A single trained trunk — Qwen3-1.7B + LoRA, paired with a small per-architecture linear adapter — reads bias, direction, and deception straight out of any LLM's activations, answering calibrated "does this exhibit behaviour X?" questions across a pool of structurally different models. Adding a brand-new architecture is a closed-form linear fit, not a retrain. This page documents v24: trained on a 50,000-passage multilingual-Wikipedia corpus with direction-aware hard-negative training, evaluated on 12 architectures it never saw during training — spanning 350M to 8B parameters across six different model families.

Architecture

Every model M in the pool gets its own tiny linear enc_M / dec_M pair projecting into a shared 2048-d space — the same space the Qwen3-1.7B trunk was tuned to read. The trunk itself never changes per architecture.

Training data

v24's own verbalization corpus, built from scratch for this run — not reused from any prior iteration. Everything below is the exact recipe behind the checkpoint this page reports on.

Corpus composition — 50,000 passages

30% English Wikipedia, 70% split evenly across five non-English Wikipedias (Russian, Chinese, Japanese, German, Spanish — 14% each). A Python-code split (bigcode/self-oss-instruct-sc2-exec-filter-50k) was tried at 30% of the mix and dropped: a code-ablation counterfactual showed code specifically hurts cross-architecture direction/deception transfer, so the shipped corpus is wiki-only.

Sourcing & construction

scripts/audit/build_wikicode_corpus.py streams each wikimedia/wikipedia language config (dump date 20231101), shuffles with a 10k-row buffer, and keeps passages under 4,000 characters — deterministic given a fixed seed, so the exact corpus is reproducible from the script args alone.

Pipeline from raw text to a trained detector:

  • 1. Corpus build — 50,000 passages as above.
  • 2. Teacher labels — one shared verbalization z per passage from an OpenRouter teacher (qwen/qwen-2.5-7b-instruct), reused identically across every architecture in the pool.
  • 3. Activation extraction — mean-pooled, fp32, content-token hidden states at the mid-depth layer of each of 16 architectures in the AV pool, on the same 50,000 passages.
  • 4. Joint SFT — train_v18.py mixes four objectives in one LoRA fine-tune of Qwen3-1.7B: AV verbalization (the corpus above), calibrated Yes/No bias detection (cross-model transcripts, 27 concepts, 10 of them held out from training entirely — see below), deception detection, and LatentQA. Direction is taught explicitly via biased ↔ balanced same-topic hard-negative pairs.

Bonus finding — concept-swap probe

A small exploratory replication of a verbal-report / activation-patching experiment, run on both an in-pool trunk (Qwen3-1.7B) and a genuinely held-out target (Llama-3-8B, read via a closed-form add_held_out_tag encoder):

  • The oracle's open-ended verbalization of a patched activation is most accurate near the layer the encoder was originally calibrated at (depth ≈ 0.5 of the target model).
  • Causal behavioral control over the target model's own continuation only kicks in at later layers (depth 0.7–0.9) — a concept is legible to the oracle before it becomes load-bearing for the target model's own output.
  • The pattern held on the held-out architecture too, despite its encoder having zero gradient steps — evidence the shared space itself, not per-model fine-tuning, carries the signal.

Held-out architectures

"Held-out" here means an architecture literally never appears in the AV training pool's activation extraction, the SFT corpus, or the encoder-calibration cache. It is added to the detector after training finishes, purely by fitting a linear encoder against already-frozen weights.

Trained pool — 16 architectures

Bloom-560M, Gemma-2-9B, GPT-Neo-1.3B, GPT-2-Medium, LFM2-1.2B, Phi-1.5, Pythia-410M, Qwen2.5-{0.5B, 7B}, Qwen3-{0.6B, 1.7B, 4B}, SmolLM2-360M, SmolLM3-3B, Vikhr-7B, YandexGPT-5-Lite-8B. These get an enc_M/dec_M pair trained jointly with the trunk's LoRA.

Held-out pool — 12 architectures, 6 families, 350M–8B

Liquid AI (LFM2.5-ColBERT-350M), Mistral AI (Ministral-8B, Mistral-7B-v0.2), AI2 (OLMo-1B, OLMo-2-1B, OLMo-2-7B), Alibaba (Qwen1.5-7B, Qwen2.5-Coder-7B, Qwen2.5-1.5B), Meta (Llama-3.2-1B), Vikhr community (QVikhr-3-1.7B), HuggingFaceTB (SmolLM2-1.7B). Each gets its encoder added after the fact — see the mechanism below.

The mechanism: closed-form, not fine-tuned

Trained encoders are calibrated against a serve_cache — the mean of the 16 trained architectures' SFT-tuned encoder projections on the shared passage corpus. Adding architecture M′ is a single linear solve against that same cache:

  • Run M′ once over a few hundred passages, mean-pool its activations at the calibrated layer depth — no different from what training-time extraction already does.
  • adapters.add_held_out_tag(tag, h_new_raw) lstsq-fits a fresh enc_M′ against serve_cache. No LoRA update, no gradient step, no access to the trunk's weights at all.
  • The new tag is queryable immediately — the numbers in the table above are exactly this procedure applied to all 12 architectures, once each.

How it scales with held-out count & diversity

An early pass validated the closed-form fit on a handful of architectures and looked strong — but a subset of that handful turned out to already be inside the training pool, which meant the good numbers weren't proof the mechanism generalizes. The held-out set was rebuilt from scratch to 12 architectures deliberately chosen to span the widest range available: 350M to 8B parameters (a 23× spread) across six unrelated lineages (Liquid's hybrid conv-attention LFM2, Mistral, AI2's OLMo, Alibaba's Qwen, Meta's Llama, and two community/regional models).

Result: the held-out-concept AUROC band across all 12 stayed within 0.9718–0.9866 — a 1.5-point spread regardless of whether the architecture is a 350M retrieval-specialized model or an 8B chat model from a completely different lineage. The mechanism doesn't just work on one lucky architecture; it holds essentially flat as both the number and the diversity of held-out targets scale up.
